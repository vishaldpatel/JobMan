## What project management methodologies and frameworks are you familiar with? Can you explain how you've applied these methodologies in your previous roles?

I'm familiar with Waterfall, Spiral and Agile methodologies including Scrum and Kanban. I have managed projects using each over my career. I'm a big believer in tailoring processes to fit the needs of the team and organization.

## Describe a particularly complex, technical project you've managed. What obstacles did you face, and how did you resolve them?

One project I delivered was a Waterfall project being implemented by a team and organization that expected it to be delivered (and reviewed / iterated upon ;)) like an Agile project - it was a full rewrite of the internal software used by the company's service department. The timeline was predetermined before requirements or scope was defined. 

Fortunately we had a fully working system to act as a benchmark and I knew the system well (I often trained people on how to use it). So, I outlined a benchmark scope as user stories ("As a user I need to log in...", "As a user, I need to see the list of...", "As a user I need to filter the list by...", etc...).

This solved many problems. It allowed us to create a "real" estimate for the project to them negotiate on scope priority. it allowed us to prioritize deliverables and product requirements. And, since the organization functioned in an Agile way, it allowed review of deliverables but also track the deliverables against the greater scope and predetermined timeline of the whole project.

## How do you measure the success of a project? What key performance indicators (KPIs) do you use, and can you share an example of a project where you achieved outstanding results?

For software projects - timeline, budget, scope (in-terms of stories and story-points), test coverage, defect rejection rate, and new bugs created. Previously a completed project was outstanding in itself since most projects tend to fail before delivery. I'm happy to say that I have delivered mostly completed projects in my career. 

With the help of AI and LLM's, we're now re-evaluating how much the KPI's can be pushed. I recently implemented a small project in two days what would have taken a developer several weeks to implement and iterate.
